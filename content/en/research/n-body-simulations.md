### Overview
During my academic research stay at the Institute of Science Tokyo, I investigate planetary orbital dynamics by simulating planet-planet scattering and evaluating test particle ejection criteria. 

### Technical Approach
* **Core Tools:** REBOUND and GENGA.
* **Integrator:** Mercurius.
* **Configuration:** Multi-body orbital simulations involving Neptune-mass planets and test particles.
* **Custom Scripts:** Developed C and Python routines to configure customized initial conditions, establish particle collision boundaries, and automate data logging.
